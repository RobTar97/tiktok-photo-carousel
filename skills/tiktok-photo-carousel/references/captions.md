## Slide copy

- **Slide 1 is the hook**: 5-9 words, creates a curiosity gap, makes sense with zero context. Examples: "Stop scrolling if you love {topic}", "This took me 47 attempts", "Wait for the last slide", "Nobody tells you this about {place}".
- **Middle slides**: one idea each, at most ~10 words, concrete (numbers, names, places beat adjectives). Highlight at most one word per slide.
- **Last slide**: the payoff plus **one** call to action: save, send to someone, comment, or "part 2?". Name a brand or place here (or on the reveal slide), not in the hook.
- Short lines read better than long ones: if a slide needs a paragraph, split it into two slides.
- Never state facts you cannot verify (prices, rankings, opening times, dates). Use only what the user or their notes provide.

## Caption

Write it to `caption.md` next to the slides.

| Part | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Hook line | 5-9 words | Searchable keyword first; mirrors slide 1 |
| Context | 1-2 sentences | What the photos do not show: where, when, backstory |
| Call to action | 1 short line | Same action as the last slide |
| Hashtags | 3-5 | Niche and place specific, no hashtag wall |

If the account is bilingual, provide each language as a separate block. Keep each caption natural in its language rather than translated word for word.

## Hashtags

Mix one broad tag (`#traveltok`), one or two niche tags (`#dreamcore`, `#hiddengems`) and one place tag (`#{city}`). Check that they match what the photos show.

## Checklist

- Hook works without context and is under ~9 words.
- Every slide is readable in about a second.
- One call to action, consistent across last slide and caption.
- No unverifiable claims; no words the brand forbids.
- Caption starts with the keyword people would search.
